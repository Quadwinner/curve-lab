# Demo video script (about 2:45)

Record at 1440p in Brave on the deployed site. Keep the live feed visible on the right. Speak slowly; each scene says what to show and what to say.

## 0:00–0:20 Hook (leaderboard hero)
**Show:** the home page hero numbers.
**Say:** "Every Meteora launchpad picks its launch settings blind. I indexed all 1.73 million Dynamic Bonding Curve launches on Solana mainnet. 39% of them were bought out within a minute, more than half come from launchpads that pre-fund every curve, and on the open market only 1.3% of tokens graduate. This is Curve Lab."

## 0:20–0:55 Leaderboard
**Show:** sort by Launches, then point at a pre-funded badge and the Holding-up column. Click "pre-funded: hide", then sort by Organic grad.
**Say:** "Configs with byte-identical settings are grouped, which turns 490,000 config accounts into 65,000 comparable setting groups. Curves completed within 60 seconds count as instant, and groups where almost every launch graduates are flagged pre-funded. Holding up shows how many graduated tokens still trade at or above their graduation price on Meteora DAMM v2. Hide the pre-funded groups and you see which settings attract real demand."

## 0:55–1:35 Setting group page
**Show:** open the top open-market group. Scroll through the price curve, fee chart, "where launches stall", the After graduation panel, and the latest launches with live progress.
**Say:** "Each setting group shows the curve, the fee schedule, and where launches stall. After graduation, we follow every token into the DAMM v2 pool it migrated to. Across all graduated tokens, 46% now trade below a tenth of their graduation price. The latest launches update from mainnet every 15 seconds."

## 1:35–2:15 Designer
**Show:** go to Designer. Change the graduation market cap and the fee decay; the charts move. Point at "Evidence from mainnet". Connect a devnet wallet and click Create config; show the explorer link.
**Say:** "Builders design new settings with Meteora's SDK and see how the closest real settings performed, with pre-funded launchpads excluded. When it looks right, export the TypeScript or create the config on devnet with one click."

## 2:15–2:35 Live feed
**Show:** the live sidebar as a new launch or graduation arrives; click its setting-group link.
**Say:** "New launches and graduations stream in from Solami as they happen, each tagged with the setting group it was launched on."

## 2:35–2:45 Close
**Show:** the GitHub README.
**Say:** "Every mainnet read goes through Solami RPC. The code is open source, and the data refreshes every six hours. Curve Lab: launch settings ranked by what actually happened. Built solo from India."
